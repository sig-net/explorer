## Goal

`DECISIONS.md` at the repository root tells a maintainer why the explorer is built as it is: each choice someone could be tempted to undo, with its reason and the measurement behind it. It stands alone once `agent-work/` is removed.

## Read first

- `agent-work/decisions.md`, whole: the source of every reason. `agent-work/architecture.md`: `## Design`, and under `## Assumptions` the lines A3 to A6 and A12, which hold the measurements.
- Nothing of the code: this document states reasons, and steps 05.3 and 05.1 own what describes the code. Where a section needs a name from the code, take it from the backend pack.

## Work

Each section says what is, why, what was measured (with the number and the size it was measured at), and what would have to be true for the choice to change. The rows in brackets are your sources, never your text.
   - `## PostgreSQL, with one schema per network` (D03, D04, D18, A5)
