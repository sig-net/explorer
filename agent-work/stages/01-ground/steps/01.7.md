## Goal

`probes/rate-limits.mjs` measures what the preprod indexer allows, and
`probes/findings/rate-limits.json` answers gate G07 with the numbers the ingestion loop's
defaults (`RANGE_SIZE`, `POLL_INTERVAL`, the page size) are chosen from.

## Read first

- `../stage.md` (the stage's file, beside `steps/`): the row G07 of the gate table.
- `../../../architecture.md`, section 5 only: count the requests one turn of the loop makes.
- `probes/findings/contract-events-paging.json` in your worktree: `max_limit_honoured`.

## Work

1. This step runs alone on purpose: every probe shares one project id, and this one spends its
   allowance. Before any code, read what Blockfrost documents for its Midnight API: requests a
   second, burst, and requests a day for the plan (its public documentation, over HTTPS). Quote
   the page and the numbers in your report. Do not call any account or billing API.
2. Run `yarn install --immutable`. Write the probe as the pack says.
3. Sustained rate. Send `{ block { height } }` at 5 requests a second for 60 seconds, then at 10
   for 60 seconds, then at 20 for 30 seconds. Stop the whole test at the first answer that is
   not 200: record its status, the NAMES and numeric values of any rate limit or `retry-after`
   header (never a header that could hold a key), and how many seconds pass before a request
   answers 200 again, asking once every 5 seconds for at most 10 minutes.
4. Range cost. With both Signet contracts, time one `contractEvents` read (first page,
   `limit` at `max_limit_honoured`) over a range of 1000, of 10000 and of 100000 blocks ending
   50 blocks below the head. Record the milliseconds and the rows of each, and whether any
   failed or timed out at 30 seconds.
5. From the above, compute and put in the evidence of G07:
   - the requests needed to catch up from block 3386746 to the head, for each range size that
     worked, at 3 requests a range (two contracts and the range's last block)
   - the requests a day one turn of the loop costs at a `POLL_INTERVAL` of 6, 15, 30 and 60
     seconds, counting the head, the checkpoint's block, and the trailing re-read of both
     contracts, beside the documented daily allowance.
6. G07 `holds` only when 5 requests a second were sustained for 60 seconds with every answer
   200 and a range of at least 1000 blocks read without failure. `fails` when either did not.
   Numbers to write: `sustained_rps` (the highest rate that ran its full time with only 200s),
   `first_refusal_status` (0 when none), `recovery_seconds` (0 when none), `range_1000_ms`,
   `range_10000_ms`, `range_100000_ms` (-1 for a range that failed), `largest_range_blocks`,
   `documented_requests_per_day` (-1 when not documented), `catch_up_requests`.
7. Run it once. Write the findings file, run `yarn format`, then `yarn lint` and
   `yarn format:check`. Commit both files. In `FINDINGS`, say which `RANGE_SIZE` and
   `POLL_INTERVAL` you would default to, and what four networks on one plan would cost a day.

## Done when

`checks/01.7.sh` proves the findings file has gate G07 with evidence and the nine numbers, and
that `holds` agrees with them. It does not run the probe again: a second burst proves nothing
the first did not, and it would throttle whoever runs next.
