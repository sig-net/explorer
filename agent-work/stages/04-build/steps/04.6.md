An expanded row verifies and shows its transactions from the inspection each lifecycle already carries. The UI no longer fetches a raw transaction, inspects one, or ships the ledger WebAssembly (D07).

- `packages/ui/src/components/midnight/use-lifecycle-verification.ts`, `use-sign-bidirectional-transaction-inspection.ts`, `sign-bidirectional-transaction-details.tsx`, with their tests.
